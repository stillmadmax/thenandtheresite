## Summary

Then & There shows you photos from your library taken on this day in earlier years and derives
trips, places and busy days from them. **Your photos never leave your device.** There are no user
accounts, no registration, no advertising, no analytics or tracking services. The only data that
leaves the device are **coordinates of place centres** for place names and **map requests** to
Apple Maps, both only when you use the map or place features.

## What the app processes

### Photos and metadata

The app reads your photo library **exclusively on the device**. From each photo and video it uses
only the capture date, the capture location (if stored in the photo), the media type, the duration
and the favorite flag. From these it builds an index stored locally in the app's storage. The images
themselves are only loaded for display. There is **no upload**, and the app **writes nothing** to
your library: no albums, no favorites, no changes.

### Place names (reverse geocoding)

For trips and places the app groups located photos into areas of about ten kilometres. To name an
area it sends **the centre point of the area (coordinates) once** to Apple's geocoding service
(`MKReverseGeocodingRequest`, a system service). The result is stored on the device and not
requested again.

| Service | Provider | Purpose | Data transmitted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reverse geocoding | Apple (MapKit, system service) | Place name and country for a photo area | Coordinates of the area centre |
| Apple Maps | Apple (MapKit, system service) | Map tiles for trip and place maps | The map region you look at |

Apple acts as an **independent controller**; Apple's privacy policy applies:
<https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>. We send **no photos, no contact data and no user or device
identifiers of our own**. Technically, your device's **IP address** is visible to Apple with every
request. The app itself stores no location history and **never requests your device's current
location**; it only uses the locations already stored inside the photos.

### Transfers to third countries

Geocoding and map tiles run through Apple system services. Apple also processes data outside the EU,
including in the USA, and states that it bases such transfers on the EU Commission's standard
contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). Where data is transferred to a country without an adequacy
decision there is a risk that authorities access the data without legal remedies comparable to EU
standards. Only area centre coordinates and map regions are transmitted.

### App Store

The app is distributed through Apple's App Store. When you download or update it, Apple processes
data (e.g. Apple Account, time of download, device data) under its own responsibility. Apple's
privacy policy applies.

### Settings and reminders

Filters, home, minimum distance and the reminder time are stored **locally on the device**
(`UserDefaults`). The daily reminder is a **local notification** scheduled on the device; no push
service is involved. All of this is deleted when you delete the app.

## Legal bases

- **Reading the library, index and settings on the device:** processing takes place exclusively
  locally and serves the function you requested - Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. You grant library access via the
  iOS system dialog, which is also consent under § 25(1) TDDDG for accessing information on your
  device. You can revoke it at any time in the iOS Settings.
- **Transmitting area centres and map regions to Apple:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, because place names
  and maps cannot be shown without these requests. They only happen when you open the trip, place or
  map screens.
- **Notifications:** consent via the iOS system dialog - Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; revocable in the iOS
  Settings or via the switch in the app.
- **Access logs of this website:** see "Hosting of this site".

## Retention

We operate no servers and store none of your data. The index, the place-name cache and the settings
stay on your device until you delete the app or re-index the library in Options. How long Apple keeps
access logs is determined by Apple. If you e-mail us, we delete the message once your request is
settled and no statutory retention obligations apply.

## Sharing photos

When you pass on a photo or video via the share function, this happens through the iOS system share
sheet with the photo's original data. You decide where to share; the chosen service is responsible
for that transfer.

## Permissions

- **Photos (All Photos)**: to read the library. With "Selected Photos" the app cannot build
  look-backs and shows a notice.
- **Notifications**: only if you turn on the daily reminder.

The app requests **no location access** and **no camera access**. Every permission can be revoked in
the iOS Settings. You are under no legal or contractual obligation to grant them; without photo
access the app simply cannot show anything.

## Data sharing and tracking

There is **no tracking**. Data is not used for advertising, not merged with third-party data and not
passed to data brokers. There are no analytics SDKs and no third-party libraries.

visitors' IP addresses for security reasons.

We have no influence on the scope and duration of this logging; GitHub acts under its own
responsibility. Details in GitHub's privacy statement:
<https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>

The **legal basis** is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to provide this mandatory
information reliably, securely and without running our own server. You may object under Art. 21 GDPR.

**Transfers to the USA:** GitHub also processes data in the United States and states that it bases
such transfers on the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR) and on the
EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

This site sets **no cookies**, embeds no external fonts, scripts or graphics and uses **no analytics
or tracking tools**. It is served over HTTPS.

## Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data processed about you (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification
(Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to
object to processing (Art. 21). Where you have given consent, you may withdraw it at any time with
effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

**Practical note:** The app creates no user accounts and no server-side database with us. All data
created in the app lives exclusively on your device; you can inspect and delete it there yourself,
at the latest by deleting the app. Since we cannot identify you from this data, under Art. 11(2) GDPR
we are not obliged to store additional data merely to fulfil these rights. If you write to the e-mail
address above, we process the details in your message - the rights apply in full there.

### Right to lodge a complaint
