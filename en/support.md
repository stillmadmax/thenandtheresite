# Support for Then & There

Then & There shows you what you photographed on this day in earlier years and recognizes trips,
places and busy days in your library. Everything happens on the iPhone; your photos never leave the
device.

## Getting started

1. **Allow access** - On first launch the app explains why it needs "All Photos", then iOS asks
   once. With "Selected Photos" the app cannot build look-backs.
2. **Wait for indexing** - The library is read once; 60,000 captures take a few seconds. A banner
   shows progress while the look-backs already fill up.
3. **Look at Today** - Every photo of this date from earlier years, sorted by year. The arrows change
   the day, tapping the date opens the date picker.
4. **Flip through** - The whole day as a story across all years. Tap right: next, left: back, hold:
   pause, swipe down: close. The year pill at the top jumps to another year.

## Photos

### Why are some tiles empty?

With "Optimize iPhone Storage" many originals live only in iCloud. The app shows the local preview
first and then downloads a small version; that needs an internet connection. Offline, such tiles stay
empty and the viewer shows "No connection".

### Are photos downloaded to my iPhone?

Only the ones you look at, at the size needed. The app keeps no image storage of its own; whatever
iOS fetches from iCloud is managed and offloaded again by the system.

### Are photos from the current year missing?

Yes, on purpose. Today shows look-backs, so earlier years only. Today's own photos are in the Photos
app.

### Why are screenshots missing?

Screenshots are hidden by default. In Options you can show them, hide videos, or show favorites only.

## Trips and places

### How does the app recognize a trip?

From photos with a location: at least two consecutive days whose photos were taken more than 50 km
from home. A day without locations in between does not break the trip. The distance can be changed in
Options.

### How does the app know where home is?

It estimates it from the places you photographed most in recent years. In Options you can set home
on the map yourself.

### Why are trips or places missing?

Photos without a location do not count. The camera only stores the location when it has location
access. Images from messengers and screenshots usually have none.

### Why is the map empty?

Map tiles come from Apple and need internet. Everything else in the app works offline.

## Reminder

The daily reminder is a local notification. It only appears on days that have look-backs and is
scheduled 60 days ahead whenever you open the app.

## Re-indexing the library

If something looks wrong, use the "Library" row with the refresh symbol in Options: the app discards
its index and reads everything again.
